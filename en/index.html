About

Control and automation engineer, graduated from IFES — Federal Institute of Espírito Santo (Brazil), with about 13 years in industry across the mining, port and steel sectors, working on maintenance, installation, programming and other project-driven activities.

Specialized in the Rockwell Automation platform (ControlLogix / Studio 5000 and FactoryTalk View), with additional experience on AVEVA, ABB and Schneider systems. My current focus is IT/OT convergence: integrating supervisory systems with MES, building C#/.NET APIs and applications, SQL Server, and the infrastructure behind these solutions.

Career scope

  • Industrial instrument calibration
  • Planning of electrical, instrumentation and automation construction work
  • Development and programming of Rockwell ControlLogix controllers and FactoryTalk View SCADA systems
  • Supervision of maintenance contracts for industrial automation teams
  • IT/OT integration between mining MES platforms and FactoryTalk View and AVEVA supervisory systems

Outside formal employment, I have developed processes for small and medium-sized craft breweries.

Available for consulting and freelance projects in automation and IT/OT integration.

Stange Convergence Lab

Stange Convergence Lab (SCL) is my initiative to bring IT advances into industrial systems: monitoring, asset management, AI and data analytics, with a focus on open source tools for the industry.

It is not a formal company yet — it is a lab where ideas are tested, documented and, when it makes sense, published in the open.

Stange-convergence-lab on GitHub

  • Monitoring

    Visibility into industrial processes and infrastructure.

  • Asset management

    Organized information about equipment and systems across their lifecycle.

  • AI

    Models and agents applied to real operational problems.

  • Data

    Collecting, integrating and analyzing industrial data.

Long-term vision

Under construction

With investment, turn SCL into a non-profit organization that creates value for industry through standardized developments and team training for new technological challenges — especially applying the advances in artificial intelligence to industrial environments.

Formats under consideration include courses and content on YouTube and LinkedIn, aiming to democratize access to industrial automation tools and education in Brazil. No dates set — next steps will be published here.
